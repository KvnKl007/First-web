Leadership

Senior Pastor and Founder

Pastor Ntwari Parfait is the visionary leader and Senior Pastor of Faith International Ministries, as well as the Executive Director of Faith Family Organization. His journey of faith began at the age of 15 while attending College George Fox in Kagarama, a school under the Evangelical Friends Church of Rwanda. It was here that Pastor Parfait's personal relationship with Christ began to take shape, as he joined the school’s prayer group and committed his life to follow Jesus. After completing secondary school, He served as an administrator in ministry for seven years, gaining valuable experience while ministering to others. During this time, he developed a deep understanding of the Word of God and embraced his pastoral calling, which is marked by a special gift for teaching. His ministry has taken him across Rwanda, where he has preached and taught the gospel in various churches and led outreach missions in markets, hospitals, schools, and universities like UR and AUCA. Pastor Parfait founded Faith Family, which later gave birth to both Faith International Ministries and Faith Family Organization. Faith International Ministries was established in November 2021 with a specific mission to manifest God’s glory in every sector of life, while Faith Family Organization provides a broader platform for outreach and community development. Currently, Pastor Parfait is pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Religious Studies at Africa College of Theology to enhance his ministerial work further. Over his decade-long journey in ministry, he has mentored countless individuals, extending his influence far beyond his local congregation. Pastor Parfait is happily married to Isimbi Yfrah, and together they serve the Lord with passion, faith, and compassion. Their partnership in ministry reflects their shared dedication to God’s work and their deep love for the Faith International Ministries community.
