Leadership

Senior Pastor and Founder

Pst.NTWARI Parfait

Pastor Ntwari Parfait is the Vision Bearer and Senior Lead of Faith International Ministries, as well as the Executive Director of Faith Family Organization. His journey of faith began at the age of 15 when he was saved and baptized, leading him to establish the Light of God prayer group during his high school years. After high school, he deepened his faith and service at Zion Temple Celebration Center and later joined the Sons of Korah Ministry, where he served as God lead. In 2021, Pastor Parfait founded Faith International Ministry and Faith Family Organization, responding to God’s call to lead and serve. He is currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Religious Studies at Africa College of Theology to further enrich his ministerial practices. Over his ten years of ministry, Pastor Parfait has mentored many people across the globe, extending his influence far beyond his local congregation. He is happily married to Isimbi Yfrah , and together, they serve the Lord with zeal, faith, and compassion within Faith International Ministries. Their partnership in ministry is a testament to their shared commitment to God's work and their deep love for their congregation.
